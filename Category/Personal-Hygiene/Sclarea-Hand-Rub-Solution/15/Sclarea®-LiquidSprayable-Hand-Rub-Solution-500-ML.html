Sclarea® Sprayable Hand Rub Solution kills 99.9% of germs instantly without water. Neem & Tulsi are natural anti-bacterial, while Aloe Vera & Glycerin leave hands soft & moisturized. For External use only. Keep away from children. Store in a cool place away from direct sunlight. It`s a highly flammable liquid, so keep away from fire.

What have we used in it :-    

  • Neem leaves extract: Neem is a natural anti-bacterial, Antiviral & anti- fungal
  • Tulsi leaves extract: Neem is a natural anti-bacterial 
  • Aloe - Vera extract: Aloe- Vera kills germs, moisturizes your hands and prevents from dryness
  • Isopropyl alcohol: Kills microorganisms anf effective against bacteria, fungi & viruses
  • Glycerin: Moisturised hands


Caution:

  • For External use only. Keep away from children. Store in a cool place away from direct sunlight.
  • It`s a highly flammable liquid, so keep away from fire. 
Precaution for use:
  • Avoid contact with eyes while using. If contact occurs, flush thoroughly with water.
  • if irritation occurs, discontinue and consult a doctor

Direction for Use (Sprayable Hand Rub Solution):-

  • For Hands:- Spray  Sclarea Hand Rub Solution on your palm and rub your hands briskly until dry.
  • For Surface/Furniture/doors etc.:-  Spray  Sclarea Hand Rub Solution on any surface, doors, handles, keys, furnitures, toys etc. and leave it for dry.
  • For Electonic items/Mobile/Toys :- Switch Off the electronic equipment. Spray Sclarea Hand Rub Solution on these equipments/toys etc. and wipe off with tissue paper or clean cloth.
  • For Delivery boxes/Shoes/Bottles/Furniters/Wallet:-Electonic items/Mobile/Toys :-  Spray Sclarea Hand Rub Solution on these  and wipe off with tissue paper or clean cloth.
  • For Vehicles:- First off the ignition of vehicle and leave it for at least 20-30 minutes so that it come on normal temperature. Spray Sclarea Hand Rub Solution inside and outside of the vehicle, wipe off with tissue paper or clean cloth.

What We Keep Out:-

  • Paraben free
  • Sulphate free
  • Ammonia free
  • Triclosan free
  • Pthalates free
