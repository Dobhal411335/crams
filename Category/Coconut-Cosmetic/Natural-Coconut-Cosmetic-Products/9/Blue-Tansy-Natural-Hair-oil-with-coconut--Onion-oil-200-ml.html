Blue Tansy 100% Natural hair oil is a wonderful combination of Coconut oil and Onion oil. Coconut oil rich in medium chain saturated fatty acids that penetrates deep into hair follicles easily and helps strengthen the cuticles, slows hair loss, promotes hair growth,protects from roughness, damage and split ends of hairs. While Onion oil that is rich in flavonoids, sulphur-rich compounds and vitamins B, C, & Folic acid; helps to strengthen strands, reduce thinning of hairs, unclog blocked roots, prevents hair loss, promotes hair growth and manage scalp infections. This oil keeps your hairs healthy, strong and lusturous naturally.

Ingredients 

  • Coconut oil
  • Onion Bulb oil

Direction for use:

  • Take a sufficient amount of oil, gently massage it onto the scalp, and work from root to tip. Leave on for 30 minutes, preferably overnight. Shampoo hair. Finger comb and air dry.
  • For best results use slightly warm oil and massage onto scalp & hair, leave overnight, and wash off in the morning.

Who can use:

  • Men and women of age 15 years and above.
  • The hair oil is suitable for all hair types.

Benefits:

  • Reduces hair fall
  • Promotes hair growth
  • Reduces dandruff
  • Protects hairs from damage & roughness
  • Prevents from breakage & split ends of hairs
  • Reduce thinning of hairs
