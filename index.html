RAY COVER CREAM

Ray Cover Cream is an advanced treatment formula that is very effective for radiation dermatitis.This cream decreases radiation-induced skin damage and promotes healing without any side effects. It helps to soothe the skin and deals with many issues of dermatitis that develop during radiation.

This cream combination is not the barrier of radiation therapy, but protect the skin from the harmful effect of radiation.

The cream is designed to moisturize damaged skin

Advanced Treatment Formula

  • The combination of key ingredients such as hyaluronic acid, almond oil, and wheat germ oil works to increase skin hydration, thereby reducing dryness and flaking
  • With ingredients like Calendula officinalis (Marigold) oil, peppermint oil, and aloe vera extract, the cream aims to calm and soothe the skin, providing relief from irritation
